> Construction Wands make building easier! With a Construction Wand you can place multiple blocks (up to 1024) at once, extending your build on the side you're facing. If that's not enough: you can upgrade your wand with additional cores, allowing you to place a block behind the block you are facing, conjure blocks in mid air or destroy lots of blocks very fast. ## 中文简介

建筑法杖：一次放下最多 1024 个方块，沿着你面对的方向延伸；换核心之后还能在背后放、空中凝块、或者快速拆一大片。搭墙、铺地板效率翻几倍。

## 在赤物语里

- 玩家侧：做杖后在物品栏选中，右键连续放置；潜行右键切换模式。
- 服务端侧：`config/constructionwand-client.toml` 是客户端设置（模式、预览），服务端无需额外配置。
