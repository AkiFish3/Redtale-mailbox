> Construction Wands make building easier! With a Construction Wand you can place multiple blocks (up to 1024) at once, extending your build on the side you're facing. If that's not enough: you can upgrade your wand with additional cores, allowing you to place a block behind the block you are facing, conjure blocks in mid air or destroy lots of blocks very fast.
